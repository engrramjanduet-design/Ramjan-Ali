Jatrabari Intersection — Dhaka–Khulna Highway (N-8)
Jatrabari, Dhaka · Client: RHD & HQ 24 ECB

Widening of a key national highway link to four lanes, with a grade-separated interchange at one of Dhaka's busiest intersections. The works involved deep sub-soil investigation and large-diameter bored piling to support the flyover structure, foundation work that had to account for both land and riverine ground conditions.

Moghbazar–Mouchak Flyover
Dhaka, Bangladesh · Client: LGED

An urban flyover built to ease traffic through one of Dhaka's most congested corridors, connecting Moghbazar and Mouchak. Construction centred on precast I-girder spans, waffle slab deck sections and abutments, with post-tensioning work to tie the precast elements into a continuous structure.
